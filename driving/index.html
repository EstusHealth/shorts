    Assessment day checklist

      Rule of 100

      1. A rep counts only if it is clean: standard instructions word for word, correct timing and scoring, no cueing, notes taken live.
      2. The last 10 reps of each drill must be clean in a row. Break the streak, keep going.
      3. Log every rep: date, partner, time taken, clean yes/no, one fix.
      4. Micro reps count. One TMT on a staff member in 5 minutes is one rep.
      5. Notes and kit setup ride along on every rep, so they stack up on their own.
      6. Practice partners (team, family, friends) are for reps only. Never write a report on them.
