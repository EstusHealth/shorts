# Fight record

A personal decision record styled as a fighter's record: past bets as wins and losses,
scheduled fights at two a year, a fight camp with next actions, and a five-voice corner
(Thinking in Bets, the Stoics, Duckworth, Goggins, Jocko).

`index.html` is the whole page. It was built as a Claude artifact and reads and saves its
data through the artifact's own store, so no record data lives in this file. Opened anywhere
else it shows a notice that the record cannot be loaded.
